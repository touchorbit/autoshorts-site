Creator workflow

Prepare and publish short-form video content from one workflow.

AutoShorts is a web-based creator tool for preparing, managing, and publishing short-form video content to social media accounts that the user explicitly connects and authorizes.

How it works

1. Prepare

Users provide or select video content that they are authorized to use and prepare it for short-form publishing.

2. Connect

Users connect supported social media accounts through the platform's official authorization flow.

3. Publish

Users review publishing settings and authorize the application to submit eligible content to their connected account.

User control and authorization

AutoShorts is intended to act only on accounts and content that users are authorized to manage. The service does not claim ownership of users' videos or third-party platform accounts.

Platform integration

Where supported, AutoShorts uses official platform APIs and authorization mechanisms. Access is limited to permissions needed for the enabled functionality and remains subject to each platform's policies, review requirements, and user consent.
