Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoShorts ("AutoShorts", "we", "our", or "the service") handles information when users access or use the service and connect third-party social media accounts.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features a user enables, the service may process:

  • Account and authorization information supplied by third-party platforms, such as platform user identifiers, basic profile information, access tokens, refresh tokens, granted permissions, and authorization status.
  • Content and publishing information, such as videos, captions, titles, publishing preferences, upload status, and platform response data.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, application logs, error records, and security events.
  • Support information voluntarily submitted by a user when requesting assistance.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the service, authenticate users, connect authorized social media accounts, prepare or publish content at the user's direction, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Third-party platforms

When a user connects a third-party platform, that platform may provide data to AutoShorts according to the permissions approved by the user. The platform's own terms and privacy policy also apply to the user's relationship with that platform. AutoShorts does not request platform permissions that are unrelated to enabled functionality.

4. Access tokens and credentials

Third-party authorization tokens, where used, are treated as confidential application data. They are intended to be stored server-side and used only to perform actions authorized by the user. Users should never send passwords for third-party platforms to AutoShorts unless a platform's official authentication flow specifically requires handling them, which the service does not intend to do.

5. Sharing of information

AutoShorts does not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only where necessary to operate the service, with connected platforms when a user initiates an authorized action, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining security and operational records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data category and platform requirements.

7. User choices and deletion requests

Users may disconnect a supported third-party account through available account controls or by revoking the application's access from the relevant platform. Users may also request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with their use of AutoShorts, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

8. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and users should protect access to their own accounts and devices.

9. Children's privacy

AutoShorts is not intended for children under the age required to independently consent to online services in their jurisdiction. The service is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information.

10. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user's country of residence when infrastructure or service providers operate internationally. Where applicable, the service will use appropriate safeguards required by law.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes or legal and platform requirements evolve. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Contact

For privacy or data requests, contact the operator through the public support channel at github.com/touchorbit. A dedicated support address may also be published here as the service enters production.
